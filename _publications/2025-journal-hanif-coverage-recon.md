Coverage-Recon closes the loop between a drone team's image-sampling motion and the 3D map being
reconstructed from those images. Rather than planning a sweep in advance, the controller reads
the evolving reconstruction and steers the fleet toward the regions where new viewpoints will
most improve the map.
