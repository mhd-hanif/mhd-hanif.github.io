Plenary talk at the 17th Multidisciplinary International Student Workshop (MISW 2026), held at the
Institute of Science Tokyo on 3–4 August 2026.

The talk traced a line through my work on multi-robot coordination — from coverage control for drone
teams building 3D maps, through to coordinated planning for autonomous excavation with fleets of heavy
machinery — and what carries over between the two: how you decide who goes where, and how you keep the
whole fleet safe while they do it.
