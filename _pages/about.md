I am a **Robotics Control Engineer** at [DeepX, Inc.](https://deepx.co.jp/) in Tokyo, where I design
coordinated planning algorithms for autonomous excavation — getting multiple autonomous shovels to
work a site safely and efficiently together.

I completed my **Ph.D. in Systems and Control Engineering** at the
[Institute of Science Tokyo](https://www.isct.ac.jp/en) (formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology) in
September 2025, advised by [Prof. Takeshi Hatanaka](https://hatanakalab.wixsite.com/website/faculty).
My dissertation, *Multi-Drone Coordinated Image Sampling for 3D Map Reconstruction through Efficient
and Scene-Adaptive Coverage Control*, developed coverage control methods that close the loop between
a drone team's motion and the 3D map it is building — validated in ROS/ROS2 and Unity, and flown in
both a controlled testbed and an agricultural farm.

During my time in the Hatanaka Laboratory I worked across a range of industry collaborations:
multi-robot task allocation for factory inspection with **Yokogawa Electric**, drone target tracking
with **Fujitsu**, and safe autonomous vessel operation in ports with **Kawasaki Heavy Industries**.
Earlier, my Master's research on predictive multi-robot task allocation for solar irradiance
monitoring was carried out with the [University of Seville](https://personal.us.es/jmaestre/)
under Prof. J. M. Maestre.

I earned my **B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering** from the
[Bandung Institute of Technology](https://www.itb.ac.id/), Indonesia, where I worked on a Hybrid
Autonomous Underwater Glider with Prof. Bambang Riyanto and led student teams building
folding-wing and hybrid VTOL UAVs. These days I'm actively exploring 3D AI — neural radiance fields,
3D Gaussian splatting and neural implicit methods — for real-time reconstruction inside robotic
control and perception loops.
