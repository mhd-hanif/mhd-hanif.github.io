Before committing a 21-metre HALE airframe to altitude, the team needed to understand what happens to a
small aircraft and its radio links on the way up. Beyond Skyline was that rehearsal.

A mini-UAV with a 1.5-metre wingspan was flown to 10,000 feet above ground level, carrying a weather
monitoring payload and a real-time video downlink. The flight validated the telemetry, tracking and streaming
chain that would later be used on the HALE aircraft.
