A convolutional neural network trained to classify brain tumours from MRI images into three classes.
The study compared several CNN architectures on a public brain tumour dataset and examined how depth and
augmentation affected accuracy on a relatively small medical dataset.

Carried out at the Biomedical Research Group at ITB and published at the World Congress on Medical Physics and
Biomedical Engineering 2018 in Prague.
